Privacy Policy

Pink Milk Marketing · Last updated September 3, 2026

Pink Milk Marketing ("we," "us," "our") provides follow-up and communication systems for home-service businesses in California. Our business address is 1031 Emerson Street, Pasadena, CA 91106. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you contact us by phone, text, email, or through the chat on this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, business name, and the details of your inquiry. We also collect standard technical information when you visit this site, such as browser type and pages viewed.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, schedule calls and appointments, provide and improve our services, and send you service updates you have requested. We do not sell your personal information.

SMS / text messaging program

Pink Milk Marketing sends conversational, scheduling and service text messages to people who have asked to hear from us. You opt in by texting DEMO (or any message) to (916) 514-6500, by giving us your mobile number during a phone call and asking for text follow-up, or by providing your number through the chat widget on this website, where this consent language is displayed.

By providing your phone number to Pink Milk Marketing through our website, by text, or by phone, you consent to receive SMS messages from us regarding your inquiry, scheduling, and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Opting out. Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at jonny@pinkmilkmarketing.com or (916) 514-6500. Opt-outs are honored immediately and permanently.

No third-party sharing. SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

Cookies

This site uses basic cookies and similar technologies for site functionality and to understand how visitors use our pages. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Data retention and security

We keep contact records only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect them. Call recordings, where made, are disclosed at the start of the call and retained for a limited period.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a revised date.

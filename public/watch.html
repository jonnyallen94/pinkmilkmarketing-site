Questions owners ask

Do I have to change my phone number or my phone system?

No. Your number stays your number. Calls ring exactly like they do today. Only the miss goes through the system.

Is this AI texting my customers?

No. Every message is written and approved before anything sends. The system picks when to send and books the calendar. It never invents a price or a promise, and the moment a customer replies, a person at your company has the thread.

Does it follow up on quotes, or only missed calls?

Both, plus your old list. Every estimate and every lead you talk to gets 30 days of follow-up. Past customers hear from you once a month with a real reason, until they book or say stop.

We already have a marketing company.

Keep them. They make the phone ring. This catches what happens when nobody answers it, and works the leads they already sent you. Their numbers look better, not worse.

We use Housecall Pro / Jobber / ServiceTitan for booking.

Good. The texts send people to your scheduler, not ours. We count the clicks and you keep booking the way you already do.

Five numbers: calls you missed, calls the system caught, conversations handed to your team, appointments booked, and a labeled estimate of jobs recovered at your average ticket. That's it.
